Once upon a time a small cat learned to count. One fish, two fish, three fish. The cat was happy.
A robot named Nia stacked blocks in a tower. Red, blue, green. The tower did not fall.
Sam baked bread. The kitchen smelled warm. He shared a slice with his neighbor.
Mia wrote a map of the river. North, then east, then a wooden bridge.
The moon was bright. Owls asked quiet questions and the trees answered with leaves.
Leo found a key under a stone. The key opened a tiny box of paper stars.
Ava counted steps to school: twenty, then thirty, then the blue gate.
Ken fixed a bicycle chain. Pedal, click, go. He rode to the library.
The baker set pies on a shelf. Apple, berry, peach. Children chose carefully.
Nora planted beans. Rain came. The beans grew taller than her boots.
Two mice argued about cheese. They split it fairly and slept well.
A lighthouse blinked twice, then once. Ships turned safely toward the bay.
The clock struck four. Tea steamed. A story began again from the start.
Pia sorted buttons by color. She made a circle and a square.
The river froze at the edges. Ducks walked on ice and laughed in duck language.
Omar drew a circle, then a triangle, then a house with a red door.
A quiet train passed the hill. Windows glowed like a string of beads.
The garden held mint and thyme. Bees worked as if they had a schedule.
Yara tied her shoes in two loops. She ran without stopping.
A small boat crossed the lake. The oars made circles that faded.
The teacher wrote numbers on the board. Two plus two is four.
Gita folded paper into a crane. The crane sat on the windowsill.
Clouds made a dragon, then a fish, then nothing at all.
The fox waited. The rabbit waited. Both went home for soup.
A lantern hung in the hallway. It made a gold square on the floor.
Ben counted backwards from ten. At one he smiled.
The old oak kept a swing. Children took turns and did not push.
Rain on the roof made a drum. The cat sat in the drum's pocket of quiet.
A letter arrived with no stamp. Inside: a drawing of a boat.
The market sold lemons and salt. Someone made lemonade and shared it.
Night came slowly. Stars appeared in the order of a song.
The last story is short. Good night. Tomorrow we will count again.
